Every personal drive in the usage report. Deleted drives still under retention are included, and
the subtitle says how many.

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Drive | The OneDrive icon (OneDrive blue), the account, named after its owner, and a link to the drive. |
| Owner | The drive's owner. |
| Storage used | Storage consumed as of the report date. |
| Quota used | How much of the drive's own storage quota is used. This is the limit a OneDrive actually stops at. |
| Files | Files stored as of the report date. |
| Active files | Files created, edited or otherwise active in the 180-day period. |
| Last activity | The most recent day with file activity. **Never** means none in the 180 days. |

**Search** matches the name, owner and id as you type, **sort** by selecting a column header,
and choose 50, 100, 250 or 500 **rows per page**. Search and sort go back to the first page.

## Near their quota

A drive is **near its quota** at 90% or more of its own storage quota. Such a drive will soon stop
syncing new files. Either clean it up, or raise its quota up to what its licence allows.
